摘要Summary

我们不收集、不出售、不共享个人数据。光灯在你的设备上运行，无需注册账号，也不接入分析、广告或崩溃上报服务。这与 App Store 隐私标签「不收集数据（Data Not Collected）」以及 App 内 PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy 的声明一致。

We do not collect, sell, or share personal data. The app runs on your device, needs no account, and does not include analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services. This matches the App Store privacy label “Data Not Collected” and the app’s PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy file.

1. 收集目的1. Purpose

光灯的功能是把 iPhone 屏幕当作分区分光工作台，并记住你在本机选择的色温、亮度、分光和光位。我们没有收集个人数据的业务目的，因此不会为注册、画像、广告、统计或风控而采集信息。

The app turns the iPhone screen into a dual-zone lighting desk and remembers the color temperature, brightness, split, and recipe you chose on this device. We have no business purpose for collecting personal data, so we do not gather information for accounts, profiling, ads, analytics, or risk control.

2. 收集项2. What we collect

我们不收集姓名、手机号、邮箱、精确或粗略位置、通讯录、相册、相机画面、麦克风、设备标识符、广告标识、浏览记录或任何可用于识别你身份的数据。App 不要求登录。

We do not collect your name, phone number, email, precise or coarse location, contacts, photos, camera frames, microphone audio, device identifiers, advertising IDs, browsing history, or any other data that identifies you. The app does not require a login.

仅保存在你设备上的内容（不属于我们收集的个人信息）：

The following stay on your device only. We do not collect them as personal information:

  • 工作台偏好：当前光位、亮度、色温、分光
  • Desk preferences: current recipe, brightness, color temperature, split
  • 是否已在本机记下 Pro 解锁状态（最终以 Apple 的购买凭证为准）
  • A local flag for Pro unlock (Apple’s purchase record is the source of truth)

工作台偏好（当前光位、亮度、色温、分光）使用系统 UserDefaults 保存在本机，对应隐私清单中的 UserDefaults API（原因代码 CA92.1）。Pro 解锁以 Apple 的购买凭证为准；本机只在 Keychain 里保存一个不透明标记，不把 Pro 状态写进 UserDefaults。卸载 App 后，这些本地记录会被删除。

Desk preferences (current recipe, brightness, color temperature, split) are stored in on-device UserDefaults, declared in the privacy manifest as the UserDefaults API (reason CA92.1). Pro unlock is determined by Apple’s purchase record. This device only keeps an opaque Keychain marker; it does not store Pro status in UserDefaults. Uninstalling the app removes these local records.

3. 使用方式3. How information is used

因为我们不收集个人数据，也就不存在将个人数据用于运营、营销或向第三方提供的情形。本机保存的工作台偏好只用于：下次打开时恢复你上次的光；以及在光位面板中显示当前正在使用的配方。

Because we do not collect personal data, we do not use personal data for operations, marketing, or third-party supply. On-device desk preferences are used only to restore your last look and to show the active recipe in the panel.

预览光位只存在于当前会话的内存中。你没有点「使用」就关掉面板，不会改写已保存的光位。

A recipe preview lives in memory for the current session. If you dismiss the panel without tapping Use, the saved recipe does not change.

4. 共享与披露4. Sharing

我们不向任何第三方共享、转让、出租或出售个人数据。我们也不把本机工作台偏好上传到自有服务器（本 App 无自建后台）。

We do not share, transfer, rent, or sell personal data. We also do not upload on-device lighting preferences to our own servers. This app has no first-party backend.

唯一相关的第三方是 Apple。当你购买或恢复 Pro 时，付款与购买凭证由 App Store / StoreKit 处理。我们收不到银行卡或 Apple ID 密码，仅能从 Apple 获知非消耗型商品 com.jiguang.pro.themes 是否已购买。

The only related third party is Apple. When you buy or restore Pro, App Store / StoreKit handles payment and the receipt. We never receive your card details or Apple ID password. We only learn from Apple whether the non-consumable product com.jiguang.pro.themes was purchased.

在法律要求、有权机关依法提出请求，或为保护用户与公众的重大合法权益所必需时，我们可能依法配合提供我们实际掌握的信息。就本 App 而言，我们实际掌握的可识别个人数据为空。

If the law requires it, a competent authority lawfully requests it, or it is necessary to protect vital interests, we may comply with information we actually hold. For this app, that identifiable personal data is none.

5. 保存与保护5. Retention and protection

我们不在云端保存你的个人数据，因此也没有云端保存期限。本机偏好保存在设备上，直到你卸载 App 或自行清除 App 数据。

We do not store your personal data in the cloud, so there is no cloud retention period. On-device preferences remain until you uninstall the app or clear its data.

若你开启了 iCloud 或电脑备份，iOS 系统备份可能包含 UserDefaults 中的工作台偏好。Pro 的 Keychain 标记使用「首次解锁后仅本机可读」，不会随 iCloud 备份到其他设备。备份由你的 Apple ID 与系统策略管理，我们无法访问这些备份。

If you use iCloud or computer backups, an iOS backup may include desk preferences in UserDefaults. The Pro Keychain marker is set to be readable on this device after first unlock and is not included in iCloud backups. Those backups are controlled by your Apple ID and system policy. We cannot access them.

我们采取的保护措施包括：不采集个人数据、不接入统计或广告 SDK、不自建账号系统、购买走 Apple 官方通道。没有任何系统能保证绝对安全；若你发现与本 App 有关的安全问题，请发邮件告知。

Our protections are structural: we do not collect personal data, we do not ship analytics or ad SDKs, we have no account system, and purchases go through Apple. No system is perfectly secure. Email us if you find a security issue related to the app.

6. 用户权利6. Your rights

在适用的个人信息保护法律下，你可能享有知情、决定、查阅、复制、更正、补充、删除、撤回同意等权利。由于我们不收集个人数据：

Under applicable privacy laws you may have rights to be informed, to decide, to access, copy, correct, complete, delete, or withdraw consent. Because we do not collect personal data:

  • 查阅 / 复制：我们没有可提供的个人数据副本。本机偏好可在 App 内直接查看和修改。
  • Access / copy: we have no personal data copy to give you. You can view and change on-device preferences in the app.
  • 更正 / 补充：请在 App 内重新调节色温、亮度、分光或光位。
  • Correction: change temperature, brightness, split, or recipe inside the app.
  • 删除：卸载光灯即可删除本机记录。购买记录由 Apple 保存，需通过 Apple 支持处理。
  • Deletion: uninstalling the app removes local records. Purchase records are kept by Apple; use Apple Support for those.
  • 撤回同意：本 App 不基于同意收集个人数据。停止使用即可。
  • Withdraw consent: the app does not collect personal data based on consent. Stop using it at any time.

行使上述权利或提出投诉，请写邮件至 weizhichao6688@gmail.com。我们会在核实后尽快回复，通常不超过 15 个工作日。

To exercise these rights or complain, email weizhichao6688@gmail.com. We will reply after verification, usually within 15 business days.

7. 儿童个人信息7. Children

本 App 的年龄分级为 4+，功能是分区分光工作台，不以收集儿童个人信息为目的，也不故意收集任何儿童的个人数据。我们不会请求监护人授权来收集儿童信息，因为我们不开展此类收集。

The app is rated 4+. It is a lighting desk. We do not aim to collect children’s personal information and do not knowingly collect anyone’s personal data. We do not ask a parent for permission to collect children’s data, because we do not collect it.

如监护人认为儿童通过非本 App 渠道（例如给客服发邮件）向我们提供了个人信息，请联系上述邮箱，我们会在核实后删除邮件中的相关信息。

If a parent believes a child sent us personal information outside the app, for example by emailing support, contact the address above. We will delete that information after verification.

系统权限System permissions

光灯不申请相机、相册、麦克风、定位、通讯录、蓝牙、追踪（ATT）或通知权限。Info.plist 中不含相机或相册的用途说明。工作台只使用屏幕发光。

The app does not request camera, photo library, microphone, location, contacts, Bluetooth, tracking (ATT), or notification permission. Info.plist has no camera or photo usage description. The desk is the screen itself.

除 Apple 系统能力外，光灯不嵌入统计分析、广告、推送、社交分享或崩溃上报 SDK。

Besides Apple system frameworks, the app does not embed analytics, advertising, push, social-sharing, or crash-reporting SDKs.
