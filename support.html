开始使用

Getting started

打不开，或打开后看不到工况条？

The app will not open, or I cannot see the job chips?

请到 App Store 更新到最新版后重开。若仍异常，重启 iPhone 再试。光灯需要 iOS 17 或更高版本。启动后主画布就是工作台：底部是工况和分光，不需要再点「开始」。

Update from the App Store and reopen. If it still fails, restart the iPhone. The app requires iOS 17 or later. After launch the canvas is the lighting desk: jobs and split sit at the bottom. There is no separate Start button.

第一次打开，界面怎么用？

What do I do on first launch?

打开后先看底部工况条：近拍、直播、夜景免费；通话和妆面随 Pro。下面是分光（配方 / 均光 / 左主 / 顶主 / 中心），再下面是色温和亮度。右上角定时器是 3-2-1 抓拍窗，半圆按钮打开光位。没有账号，也没有设置页。

Start with the shot-job chips: Close-up, Live, and Night are free; Call and Makeup need Pro. Below that is split lighting (Recipe / Even / Left key / Top key / Center), then temperature and brightness. The timer is the 3-2-1 capture window; the half-circle opens recipes. No account, no settings screen.

如何切换语言？

How do I change the language?

界面语言跟随 iPhone 系统语言，App 内没有语言开关。本网站可用右上角「中 / EN」切换。英文名是 Guangdeng。

The app follows the iPhone system language. There is no in-app language switch. This website can switch with 中 / EN. The English name is Guangdeng.

有安卓版吗？

Is there an Android version?

目前只有 iOS。请从 App Store 下载，App ID 6811735744。

iOS only for now. Download it from the App Store, app ID 6811735744.

工况与亮度

Jobs and brightness

屏幕不够亮，或会自动变暗？

The screen is not bright enough, or it dims by itself?

把亮度滑条拉高，并保持 App 在前台。系统自动亮度或低电量模式可能压低观感，可在「设置 › 辅助功能 / 显示与亮度」里临时关闭自动亮度后再试。抓拍窗会先收亮再给峰值，那是设计，不是故障。

Raise the brightness slider and keep the app in the foreground. Auto-Brightness or Low Power Mode can dim the look. Temporarily turn off Auto-Brightness in Settings, then try again. The capture window dims first, then peaks — that is intended.

离开 App 后，系统亮度会怎样？

What happens to system brightness when I leave?

回到主屏幕、切到后台或划掉 App 时，光灯会把系统亮度恢复为进入前的状态，避免把工作台亮度留在系统里。

When you go Home, background the app, or swipe it away, Guangdeng restores the system brightness from before you opened it, so the desk level is not left behind.

色温滑条怎么理解？

How does the temperature slider work?

滑向一侧更冷，另一侧更暖，作用在当前光位的底色上。和亮度一样，调节后会立刻保存在本机。

One end is cooler, the other is warmer. It tints the current recipe. Like brightness, changes are saved on device immediately.

工况、分光和抓拍窗是做什么的？

What are shot jobs, split lighting, and the capture window?

工况一键套上配方、色温和分光几何。分光让屏幕按左主右辅、顶光或中心追光铺光，不是整屏同一颜色。抓拍窗先倒计时收亮，再给约 1.6 秒峰值，方便用另一部手机或后置相机拍。光灯自己不申请相机。

A shot job writes a recipe, sliders, and lighting geometry in one tap. Split lighting paints left-key, top-key, or center-spot — not one flat color. The capture window counts down, then peaks for about 1.6 seconds so a second phone or the rear camera can shoot. Guangdeng does not request the camera.

光位配方

Recipes

光位只能预览不能用？

I can preview a recipe but cannot apply it?

免费光位（近拍平光、夜景辅光）点「使用」即可。带锁的光位需解锁 Pro；可先预览分光，点「预览 · 需 Pro」或「使用」会进入购买。

Free recipes (Close-up Flat, Night Fill) apply when you tap Use. Locked recipes need Pro. You can preview the split first. Tapping the locked action opens the purchase sheet.

预览会不会把原来的光改掉？

Does preview replace my saved look?

不会。预览只在当前面板有效。不点「使用」就关掉面板，会回到原来的光位。

No. Preview lasts only while the panel is open. Dismiss it without Use and the previous recipe returns.

免费和 Pro 光位分别有哪些？

Which recipes are free or Pro?

免费：近拍平光、夜景辅光。Pro：窗边冷辅、妆面柔粉、中心追光、左主右辅、通话冷白、顶光轮廓、证件平光。通话和妆面工况也随 Pro 解锁。与 App 内名称一致。

Free: Close-up Flat, Night Fill. Pro: Window Cool, Makeup Rose, Center Spot, Key Left, Call Cool, Top Rim, ID Flat. Call and Makeup jobs unlock with Pro. Names match the app.

购买与恢复

Purchases

会不会要订阅？

Is this a subscription?

不会。Pro 是一次买断的非消耗型商品，不是订阅。价格以 App Store 购买框的 StoreKit 价格为准，网站不写死金额。

No. Pro is a one-time non-consumable, not a subscription. The App Store sheet shows the StoreKit price. This site does not hard-code an amount.

购买没成功，或提示失败？

The purchase failed or was cancelled?

确认网络正常，用你的 Apple ID 完成系统购买框。若你点了取消，App 会提示「已取消」。真正失败会提示「购买没成功，再试一次」。仍不行：退出 App Store 账号后重登，或稍后再试。

Check the network and complete the system purchase sheet with your Apple ID. Cancel shows “purchase cancelled”. A real failure shows “purchase didn’t go through, try again”. Still stuck: sign out of the App Store and back in, or try later.

换机或重装后光位没了？

Recipes disappeared after a new phone or reinstall?

打开光灯 › 右上角光位 ›「解锁全部」或任意锁定光位 › 在购买半屏点「恢复购买」。必须使用购买时的同一个 Apple ID。

Open the app › recipes (top-right) › Unlock all or any locked recipe › tap Restore Purchases on the sheet. Use the same Apple ID that bought Pro.

提示购买正在等待批准？

It says the purchase is waiting for approval?

这通常出现在「购买前询问」（Ask to Buy）或家庭共享场景。请让批准人在其设备上处理请求。批准前锁定光位不会解锁。

This usually means Ask to Buy or Family Sharing. The approver must handle the request on their device. Locked recipes stay locked until it is approved.

买完 Pro 还要再点一次吗？

After buying Pro, do I need another tap?

购买成功后会提示「已解锁，主题可用了」，锁定会解开。若当时在预览某个 Pro 光位，仍需再点「使用」才会保存该光位。

Success shows that Pro is unlocked and recipes are ready. Locks are removed. If you were previewing a Pro recipe, tap Use once more to save that recipe.

隐私与权限

Privacy

收不收集我的数据？要相机吗？

Do you collect my data? Do you need the camera?

不收集个人数据，也不需要相机或相册权限。工况、分光与抓拍窗都在设备本地完成。光灯不是相机 App，也不会美颜或读取照片。详见隐私政策和用户协议。

We do not collect personal data and do not need camera or photo library access. Jobs, split lighting, and the capture window stay on device. This is not a camera and does not beautify or read photos. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
