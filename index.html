先选工况，再铺光

Pick the job, then paint the light

光灯不是相机，也不是手电筒色块包。不美颜、不读相册，只用屏幕按几何铺光。

Not a camera and not a flashlight skin pack. No beauty filters, no photo access — the screen is painted as lighting geometry.

拍摄工况 Shot jobs

近拍、直播、夜景免费；通话和妆面随 Pro 光位解锁。一键套上配方、色温和分光。

Close-up, Live, and Night are free. Call and Makeup unlock with Pro. One tap writes recipe, sliders, and split.

分区分光 Dual-zone geometry

配方、均光、左主、顶主、中心。屏幕按几何铺光，不是整屏同一颜色。

Recipe, Even, Left key, Top key, Center. The screen is painted as geometry, not one flat color.

抓拍窗与光位 Capture window and recipes

3-2-1 后给约 1.6 秒峰值亮，方便另一只相机拍。打开光位后面板会写清当前配方用途，预览分光后再「使用」。

A 3-2-1 window peaks for about 1.6s for a second camera. Open Recipes: the panel states the selected purpose; preview the split, then Use.

九个带用途的光位

Nine recipes with a purpose

免费两款日常光位；其余七款由 Pro 一次买断解锁，不是订阅。点开卡片，面板会写清当前怎么用，不是换皮色块。

Two everyday recipes are free. The other seven unlock with Pro, a one-time purchase. Tap a card and the panel states how to use it — not a recolored swatch.
