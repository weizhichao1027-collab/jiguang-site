光灯

打开就是补光灯。自拍、直播、短视频缺光时，把屏幕对着自己或镜头即可。

Open and you are lit. For selfies, livestreams, and short videos, point the screen at your face or the lens.

在 App Store 下载 Download on the App Store 先看怎么用 See how it works

打开就能用，不用学参数

Ready on launch, no lighting school

光灯不是相机，也不会美颜或读取相册。它只用屏幕铺一层稳定的光。

This is not a camera. It does not beautify or read your photos. It uses the screen as a steady lamp.

打开即补光 Fullscreen on launch

启动后整块屏幕就是补光灯。把手机对着自己或镜头，不必外接灯具。

The whole screen becomes the fill light. Point the phone at yourself or the lens. No extra gear.

色温与亮度 Temperature and brightness

底部两条滑条分别调冷暖和明暗。使用中请保持 App 在前台。

Two sliders at the bottom set warmth and intensity. Keep the app in the foreground while you shoot.

先预览，再使用 Preview, then apply

点右上角打开主题。点选即可预览；只有点「使用」才会保存。点错也能回到原来的光。

Open themes from the top-right. Tap to preview. Only Use saves the look. Dismiss the panel to keep the previous light.

Pro 一次买断，不是订阅

Pro is a one-time unlock, not a subscription

基础补光和柔白日光、夜拍暖光免费。Pro 解锁其余场景主题；用购买时的同一个 Apple ID 即可恢复购买。

Core lighting plus Soft Daylight and Night Warm Light stay free. Pro unlocks the remaining scenes. Restore with the same Apple ID you used to buy.

  • 商品：全部场景主题（非消耗型）
  • Product: all scene themes (non-consumable)
  • 付款由 Apple 处理，价格以购买框为准
  • Apple processes payment. The sheet shows the price.
  • 无需注册账号，也无需相机或相册权限
  • No account. No camera or photo library permission.
